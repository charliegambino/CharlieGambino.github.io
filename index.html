MADE ON
THE INTERNET.

THE FOLLOWER draws from the strange visual language of the internet — forgotten websites, old games, strange videos, urban legends, screenshots, movie scenes, digital memories, and the endless collection of things people leave behind online.

It is part story, part experiment, part reflection on the world that exists behind the screen.
